Orders

Orders are accepted subject to stock confirmation. Product compatibility information must be checked before purchase where relevant, especially for CarPlay/Android Auto accessories.

Price

Consumer-facing prices are displayed in GEL. If PayPal is used, the actual PayPal charge may need to be processed in a supported PayPal payment currency such as USD; the checkout must show that amount before confirmation.

Seller information

Complete the seller's legal name, registration/tax ID, physical/legal address and contact details before going live.
