Cut-off

Same-day target cut-off: 15:00. This value is configurable and must be aligned with supplier and courier operating hours before launch.

Regions

Regional orders are handed to a courier partner after stock confirmation. Final delivery SLA and tariffs must be confirmed with the selected carrier.
